# How to Play

Introduction to Data Science / Part B

**Ocean Rescue – The Lost Ocean World**

P Sindhuja (EC24B1042) · S Shamiksha (EC24B1047) · Ch Blessy (EC24B1055)

Play as a marine explorer and use simulated ocean data to decide where cleanup should start. Click Play Game to choose a level. Completing a level unlocks the next one.

| **Control** | **Action** |
| --- | --- |
| Arrow keys | Swim up, down, left, or right. |
| Spacebar | Press near a bottle or piece of plastic to collect it. Hold near the turtle to free it. |
| P / M | Pause or resume swimming / mute or unmute sound. |

## Level 1 — Ocean Explorer

Collect five water samples. Swim close to each bottle and press Spacebar. Read the temperature, dissolved oxygen, and plastic pollution in the sample message. Avoid rocks and jellyfish. Refill oxygen by swimming near the surface or through an oxygen bubble. Jellyfish cost one life and lower oxygen.

## Level 2 — Clean the Data

Correct the sample dataset. Choose Keep for valid rows, Remove row for duplicates, and Fill with median for missing or impossible readings. Click Check Cleaning and use the hint to fix any highlighted rows.

## Level 3 — Ocean Investigation

Find the region with the highest average plastic pollution. Click Dive & Sample for all six regions. Use the variable dropdown to see the mean and median, and read the correlation table. Select the region with the highest mean plastic value and click Submit.

## Level 4 — Pollution Detective

Spot unusual readings. Mark any of the 30 pollution values that look unusual. Click Run IQR & Z-score to see the flagged values, quartiles, IQR limits, mean, and standard deviation. Review the results and click Continue.

## Level 5 — Visualisation Lab

Match each task to a graph. Use a bar chart to compare six regions, a line graph to show changes over nine surveys, and a scatter plot to compare dissolved oxygen with plastic pollution. Preview each graph and click Submit Graph.

## Level 6 — Marine AI Prediction

Train the Linear Regression model, review the line, equation, MAE, and R², and use the slider to predict pollution for surveys 10–15. Click Challenge B and train the Logistic Regression model. Review loss and accuracy. Change temperature, oxygen, and pollution to see the risk percentage and Healthy or At Risk label. Click Finish level.

## Level 7 — Final Mission

Collect five bottles and hold Spacebar near the trapped turtle until the rescue bar fills. Avoid jellyfish and refill oxygen as needed. In the final table, select the region with the highest mean plastic pollution and the region with the highest predicted risk. Click Start restoration. Both answers must be correct to trigger the animation.
