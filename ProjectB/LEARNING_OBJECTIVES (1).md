# Learning Objectives

Introduction to Data Science / Part B

**Ocean Rescue – The Lost Ocean World**

P Sindhuja (EC24B1042) · S Shamiksha (EC24B1047) · Ch Blessy (EC24B1055)

Students practise the following data science skills using simulated measurements of ocean conditions.

- **Level 1 Data collection —** Identify temperature, dissolved oxygen, and plastic pollution as measurements recorded in a water sample.

- **Level 2 Data cleaning —** Identify duplicates, missing values, and impossible readings. Decide whether to keep a row, remove a duplicate, or fill a value with the median.

- **Level 3 Exploratory data analysis —** Use the mean and median to summarise measurements and compare regional averages. Read correlations to describe how strongly variables move together and in which direction.

- **Level 4 Outlier detection —** Use quartiles and the interquartile range (IQR) to identify readings outside the usual range. Use the mean and standard deviation to interpret Z-scores and compare what the two methods flag.

- **Level 5 Data visualisation —** Use a bar chart to compare regions, a line graph to show trends over time, and a scatter plot to explore how two measurements relate.

- **Level 6 Machine learning —** Use Linear Regression to predict a pollution value for a future survey. Use Logistic Regression to estimate risk and classify ocean conditions as Healthy or At Risk.

- **Level 7 Cleanup decisions —** Compare the region with the most plastic pollution with the region at highest predicted risk. Explain how plastic pollution, dissolved oxygen, and other conditions can inform cleanup priorities.
